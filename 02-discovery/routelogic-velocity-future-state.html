Future-State Journey · Initiative Brief

RouteLogic Velocity — From Shadow Workflow to a Single Trustworthy Board

Persona
Mid-size 3PL dispatcher — keeps a live fleet routed correctly and knows, in real time, where every stop stands.
Goal
Reassign routes and read accurate delivery status off one trustworthy board — without a phone in the other hand.
Strategy
Reclaim operational simplicity: strip legacy noise and make the tool the fastest path for everyday work.
Today's Workaround (replaced)
A parallel WhatsApp group + voice calls run as "the real system" alongside a board they've learned to distrust.

Competitive Advantages Over the Manual Workaround

vs. Double Entry

One entry propagates instantly — no re-typing every reassignment into WhatsApp.

vs. Manual Polling

The board self-updates in real time, ending the ping-each-driver status chase.

vs. Lost Data

Every route & POD stays on-platform — structured, auditable, feeding admin reporting.

Value prop: RouteLogic Velocity — a streamlined interface that eliminates operational noise and manual workarounds while preserving backend platform power, defending enterprise contracts against leaner rivals.
